# Crouch End Station

[Crouch End Station](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/crouch-end-station-d2651a5550f34d80b0dba6e971f3a1ae)
by [artfletch](https://sketchfab.com/artfletch).
Licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Photogrammetry captured in May 2022 using 2,821 photographs, Sony a7R III,
processed in RealityCapture. The public model API reports 1,907,390 faces and
956,731 vertices. These counts describe the published model, not a performance
measurement or a verified physical scale.

Preparation embeds the downloaded glTF buffers and textures into a GLB without
simplifying geometry or resizing textures. Placement and physical scale are
explicit environment metadata. There is one asset for all devices.
