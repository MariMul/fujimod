FujiMod is a day old unauthorized firmware modification for Fujifilm X-series cameras. It has features like aspect ratio crop (), highlight metering, snap focus, unlimited recipes (that save white balance & shift), more film simulations ( ETERNA 👍, 🤷) and more. Although it currently supports the cameras listed below, we have a plan to eventually support all camera models.

FujiMod is not ready for your camera. It is too new and changing too quickly. I’ll release SD card dual boot and then installable firmware as I gain confidence in our design and simulation testing system.

Until then, you can try it in the camera emulator below.

-Jake
